Brand Lab · 10 Concepts

One name, ten marks.

Logo explorations for Darlene.Design — each concept pulls from a different side of the brand: calm geometry, the designer-detective, electric mobility, and living design systems. All marks are pure SVG, drawn on a 64px grid, in ink & ultramarine.

Darlene.Design
CONCEPT 01

Eclipse

A full circle with a quiet circular bite — the negative space reads as a crescent "D". Calm, planetary, and already echoed by the orbit motif on the site. The small blue satellite is the designer in orbit.

16px · dark · brand
Darlene.Design
CONCEPT 02

Orbits

Three overlapping rings — research, design, and systems — sharing one center line. Lifted straight from the site's hero illustration, so brand and site become one continuous idea. The middle ring wears the blue.

16px · dark · brand
CONCEPT 03

The Period

A pure typographic logo: bold grotesk "Darlene", an oversized ultramarine full stop, and a serif italic "d." as the echo. The period is the brand — a designer who finishes sentences, decisively and calmly.

favicon "D." · dark
Darlene.Design
CONCEPT 04

Double D

Darlene + Design: two D's mirrored across a hairline of white space, one ink and one ultramarine. Together they form an open gate — design as the doorway between people and complex systems.

16px · dark · brand
Darlene.Design
CONCEPT 05

Detective Lens

The designer-detective, literally: a "D" whose bowl is a magnifying glass, with a blue handle striking out at 45°. For the designer who observes everyday frictions with fresh eyes before ever drawing a screen.

16px · dark
Darlene.Design
CONCEPT 06

Token Matrix

A "D" assembled entirely from dots — design tokens forming a letterform, the way a design system assembles products. One token turns blue: the semantic accent. Feels at home next to Storybook and Figma Variables.

16px · dark
Darlene.Design
CONCEPT 07

Charge

A solid ink "D" carrying a bolt of ultramarine — seven years of design for electric mobility, batteries, and energy networks, condensed into one glyph. The most industry-specific mark of the set.

16px · dark · brand
Darlene.Design
CONCEPT 08

The Route

One continuous stroke draws a lowercase "d" like a navigation route, ending at a blue destination pin — a nod to the scooter navigation work where the journey began. The fading dots are the road already travelled.

16px · dark
Darlene.Design
CONCEPT 09

Interface Stack

Three interface cards fanned like a hand of well-organized work — the front one alive in blue with a tiny UI sketched on it. Reads instantly as "product designer", and animates beautifully as a loading mark.

16px · dark
Darlene.Design
CONCEPT 10

Signature

A single flowing hand-drawn "d" with a flick of a tail and a floating blue dot — the most personal mark of the ten. Where the others speak of systems, this one signs the work: made by a human, with care.

16px · dark
How to choose

A quick lens: Eclipse (01) and Orbits (02) are the safest brand fits — they already live in the site's visual language. The Period (03) is the most timeless and works everywhere text works. Detective Lens (05) and Signature (10) carry the most personality; Charge (07) ties hardest to the EV industry — which also means it dates fastest if the industry changes.
